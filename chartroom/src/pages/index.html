How location is used
  • Off until you switch it on

    Nothing asks for your location when the page loads.

  • Your browser asks first

    You can refuse, and the built-in example airports are used instead.

  • Coordinates stay on your device

    Chartroom has no server to send them to. The nearby airports are picked inside your browser.

  • Clear it any time

    The privacy page has a button that deletes the saved airport list.
