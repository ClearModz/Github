Aeronautical data

FAA VFR Sectional Charts
The chart on the left map. Published by the Federal Aviation Administration, Aeronautical Information Services, and republished every 56 days. The copy shown here can lag the current edition.
FAA Class Airspace
The airspace boundaries, floors and ceilings on the right map and in the quiz. Used as served by the FAA’s public data service.
OurAirports
Airport names, locations, elevations, runways and frequencies, in a trimmed US extract built into this site (see the retrieved date in the data file). Public domain. It is community data and can be out of date. Check official sources.
NOAA Aviation Weather Center
METAR and TAF reports. Public data from the National Weather Service.
FAA temporary flight restrictions
The TFR list. NOTAMs are linked, not shown, because the FAA NOTAM service needs registration.
