What this site collects

Chartroom has no accounts and no server of its own that stores information about you. It sets no cookies.

Your location

Location is off until you turn on Use my location on any page with the switch (Sectional Practice, Airports, Plan). Your browser then asks for permission, and you can refuse.

If you allow it, the page works out which airports are near you inside your browser. Your coordinates are not sent to Chartroom or to any service it uses.

To avoid downloading the airport list again, the page keeps your position rounded to a tenth of a degree (roughly 7 miles) in your browser. Plans you save and aircraft data you enter are also kept in your browser only. This button deletes all of it.

Services your browser contacts

The map pages load data straight from other services. Each one can see your IP address and browser details, as any website can, and handles them under its own policy. Fonts, icons and the map libraries are served from this site, not from a third party.

  • FAA services on arcgis.com supply the sectional chart tiles and the airspace data. Requests include which tiles or which map area you are viewing.
  • Amazon Web Services supplies terrain tiles. Requests include tile numbers.
  • OpenFreeMap supplies the base map under the airspace. Requests include tile numbers.
  • MapLibre demo servers supply the lettering for airspace labels. Requests do not include a map position.
  • aviationweather.gov (NOAA Aviation Weather Center) supplies METAR and TAF reports. Requests include the airport codes you are viewing, not your position.
  • tfr.faa.gov supplies the list of temporary flight restrictions. The request is the same for everyone.
  • The airport list is built into this site. It is not downloaded from anywhere else.

Your choices

  • Turn Use my location off at any time. The built-in example airports are used instead.
  • Use the button above to clear what the page saved.
  • Revoke the permission from the lock icon beside the address bar.
  • Browsers also let you clear all site data for Chartroom.

Changes to this page

If Chartroom starts using accounts, analytics or another service, this page will be updated first. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
